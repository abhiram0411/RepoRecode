# Community conduct

Treat contributors and learners with respect. Constructive technical disagreement is welcome. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, disclosure of private data and shaming learners are not acceptable.

Use clear examples, acknowledge uncertainty, and focus criticism on behavior and code. Maintainers may remove harmful content and restrict participation proportionately. Report sensitive concerns privately to the project owner through the distribution channel. A dedicated community reporting address must be configured before public launch.
